# eng.rst.oll

### RST Online-Learning

RST Corpus of online learning discussion messages

This corpus consists of 327 Rhetorical Structure Theory (RST) analyses of messages posted during online discussions that took place in courses at Nova Southeastern University between 2003 and 2005. The analyses were created in 2006. Participants have been anonymized. The conferencing software used in the courses included Allaire Forums and WebCT.

The analyses are originally in RS3 format, compatible with RST-Tool and rstWeb.

To get a copy of the complete corpus in zip format, contact the author.

For more info on the discussions:

Potter, A. (2008). Interactional coherence in asynchronous learning networks: A rhetorical approach. _The Internet and Higher Education_, 11, 87–97.

Potter, A. (2007). An investigation of interactional coherence in asynchronous learning environments Nova Southeastern University]. Fort Lauderdale, Florida. 

Potter, A. (2009). Constructive chaos: Topic management in asynchronous learning networks. The International Journal of Technology, Knowledge and Society, 5(3), 1-12.

### Notes on Segmentation

This dataset does not contain discontinuous discourse units (split 'same-unit').

### Notes on Relation Classification

The original data contains 7 multinuclear and 26 satellite-nucleus relations, which can be found in the original label column. The converted data has the final label harmonized with the reduced shared task label set.
